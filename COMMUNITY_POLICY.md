# STU Mobile Wrapper Free Community Policy

STU Mobile Wrapper is distributed by Starlight Unit Studios as a free source-available community project.

For the official STU Mobile Wrapper project, the current policy is:

- no purchase price for the wrapper
- no paid wrapper license tier
- no wrapper subscription fee
- no paid official white-label wrapper service
- no donation mechanism tied to wrapper licensing
- no license phone-home
- no installation reporting or user-count telemetry

Individuals, communities, businesses, educational institutions and other organizations may use the wrapper without a wrapper license fee while respecting the STU Mobile Wrapper Community Source License.

Generated applications may be commercial. They may use their own branding and may generate revenue from their own app functionality, content or services. This policy does not require an app publisher to make unrelated application code, web content or backend services public merely because an unmodified wrapper build is used.

The official project does not grant a general right to operate a paid wrapper-generation or white-label wrapper service using this code. Individual written exceptions are handled separately under the License and are never automatic.

This policy describes the official project's present operation. It does not replace `LICENSE.de.md` or `LICENSE.md`, and it is not a promise concerning unrelated Starlight Unit Studios works.
